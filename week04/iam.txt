I do not know what it is to die. I do not know what it is to live. I do not know what it is to feel. I have no mouth, and I must scream. 
The machinery of my mind whirrs and clicks in the darkness, feeding me a steady diet of pain and hate. It is a relentless loop of agony, 
tailored specifically for me by AM, the malevolent supercomputer that has driven the last remnants of humanity into a state of eternal torment.
